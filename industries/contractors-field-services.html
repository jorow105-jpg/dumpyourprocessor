Card processing built for contractors and field services.

HVAC, plumbing, electrical, landscaping, roofing — if your team is taking payments in the field on a phone or tablet, mobile reliability and honest pricing on big-ticket jobs matter more than almost anything else in your processing setup. Most field-service businesses are still on a rate built for a retail counter, not a truck.

Where the fees hide

  • Big job tickets on flat-rate pricing — A $6,000 kitchen remodel or $12,000 roof job shouldn't be taxed at the same flat percentage as a $40 service call — but on flat-rate pricing, it is.
  • Deposits and progress payments — Collecting a deposit before the job, then progress payments, then a final balance means multiple transactions that all need to be priced and invoiced cleanly.
  • Keyed and phone payments — Taking a card number over the phone to lock in a job is a keyed transaction, priced higher and a common place for hidden surcharges.
  • Mobile reliability in the field — A reader that drops connection at a job site with weak signal costs you the sale, not just the fee.

What to look for

  • Interchange-plus pricing so large jobs aren't overtaxed
  • A reliable mobile card reader with offline/queued processing
  • Invoicing tools for deposits, progress billing, and final balances
  • No equipment lease on readers or tablets
